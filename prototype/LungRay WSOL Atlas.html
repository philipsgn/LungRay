Multi-Task CXR CADx, Weakly-Supervised Localization & Grounded Clinical RAG

LungRay unifies thoracic multi-label diagnosis (14 NIH pathologies), Tuberculosis screening, and COVID-19 triage under a single anatomically-gated multi-task architecture. Below is the interactive blueprint of our three-stage production pipeline: Data Preparation & Anatomical Gate → Multi-Task 3-Head Model Extraction → Quantitative WSOL Evaluation, 5-Tier Safety Guards & Grounded Clinical RAG.

1. End-to-End Multi-Task Project Pipeline

Every radiograph flows through deterministic data hygiene, anatomical gating, masked multi-task representation learning, and a five-tier post-hoc clinical defense layer before reaching the PACS workstation.

Stage I · Data Preparation & Anatomical Gate Zero Leakage

6 Clinical Datasets (135K+ CXRs) → 512×512 Letterbox → Frozen Dense-UNet Gate
  • Strict Patient & Source Isolation: NIH ChestX-ray14 (112,120 images) split 70/10/20 by Patient_ID (Train ∩ Test = ∅); COVIDx CXR-4 & Qatar Radiography stratified by acquisition data_source and patient group; expert bounding boxes (VinDr-CXR, RSNA) reserved 100% for evaluation (Rule 4).
  • Medical Preprocessing & Balanced Sampler: Aspect-ratio preserving 512×512 letterboxing, CLAHE contrast enhancement, safe affine rotation (±7°; vertical flip strictly prohibited), and WeightedMultiTaskBatchSampler enforcing a fixed 60% NIH : 25% COVID : 15% TB batch ratio.
  • Frozen Dense-UNet Anatomical Gate: Trained on 3,014 paired radiographs/masks (Validation Dice 0.9630, IoU 0.9300). Applies Dilated Soft Masking (6% dilation, 10% background attenuation) with a <5% White-out Lung Fail-Safe bypass so total pleural effusion or consolidation is never masked out.

Stage II · Multi-Task 3-Head Model Extraction Masked Backprop

Shared Encoder (DenseNet-121 / ResNet-50 / ViT) → LayerNorm → 3 Isolated Heads
  • Shared Backbone & Parameter-Free Neck: Extracts 1024×16×16 spatial feature maps at 512×512 resolution (1 cell = 32×32 px), followed by Global Average Pooling, parameter-free LayerNorm(elementwise_affine=False), and Dropout(p=0.20).
  • Three Specialized Heads & Masked Loss Engine: Head A (14 NIH thoracic pathologies — Asymmetric Focal Loss γ-=4.0, γ+=0.0, clip=0.05); Head B (Tuberculosis binary screening — prevalence-weighted BCE); Head C (COVID-19 binary screening — class-weighted BCE). Each sample updates only its active head and shared encoder (Zero Cross-Head Gradient Leakage).
  • Two-Stage Fine-Tuning & Calibration: Stage 1 head warmup (frozen encoder) → Stage 2 differential cosine-annealing fine-tuning with Exponential Moving Average (EMA), 3-Pass Clinical TTA (Original, Horizontal Flip, CLAHE), and per-class Youden's J threshold calibration (τ_c = argmax(TPR - FPR)).

Stage III · Evaluation, 5-Tier Safety & Grounded RAG 176+ Tests PASS

WSOL Box-IoU & Shortcut Audit → Helmholtz OOD Lockout → ICD-10 Grounded RAG
  • Classification & WSOL Benchmarks: Reaches 0.8247 Macro AUROC on NIH-14 (20-11 baseline Val/Test 0.7858/0.7508), 0.9986 Test AUROC on Tuberculosis (AG-MTCNet 512×512), 95.43% external retention on Stanford CheXpert, 0.7631 Mean Box-IoU on RSNA Pneumonia, and 0.6049 Box-IoU on VinDr-CXR without bounding-box training supervision.
  • Shortcut Mitigation & 5-Tier Clinical Guards: Clinical Pulmonary Grounding boosts COVID-19 in-lung Grad-CAM energy from 38.49% to 94.92% (p = 1.15×10⁻²⁶). Post-hoc Helmholtz Free Energy (τ_E = 9.0421) & Shannon Entropy (τ_H = 0.6275) reject corrupted/OOD scans (1.0000 OOD AUROC), while the Smart Clinical Guardrail automatically downgrades Head C flags if in-lung energy drops below 80%.
  • Deterministic Grounded Clinical RAG: Retrieves verified ICD-10 codes, Fleischner Society / WHO radiographic hallmarks, and follow-up triage pathways linked to Grad-CAM anatomical zones with zero hallucination and automatic OOD lockout.

4. Production Model Registry, Quantitative Benchmarks & 5-Tier Safety

LungRay serves five production model checkpoints on the FastAPI + Next.js PACS workstation. Each model addresses a clinical triaging role, backed by quantitative evaluation on NIH ChestX-ray14, TB Radiography, COVIDx CXR-4, VinDr-CXR, RSNA, and Stanford CheXpert.

1. Standard CADx (SOTA Baseline)

DenseNet-121 · checkpoints/best_model_20_11.pth
  • High Discrimination: Validation Macro AUROC 0.7858 on NIH-14, 0.9966 on TB, and 0.9786 on binary COVID (Mean AUC 0.9203).
  • Independent Test: Held-out NIH-14 AUROC 0.7508 (N=25,596); TB AUROC 1.0000; COVID AUROC 0.7692.
  • Clinical Role: Fast primary multi-task triage with calibrated Youden's J decision thresholds across all 16 pathologies.

2. AG-MTCNet 512×512 (Anatomical Gate)

Dense-UNet (Dice 0.963) → DenseNet-121 (512 px) · ag_mtcnet_best.pth
  • Anatomically Gated: Frozen Dense-UNet gate filters non-pulmonary background noise with 6% dilation and 10% attenuation.
  • Tuberculosis SOTA: Achieves 0.9986 Test AUROC on TB screening with near-perfect specificity.
  • Smart Guardrail for Head C: Calibrated Youden threshold (τ = 0.6123) with automated clinical down-flagging when in-lung energy < 80%.

3. WSOL Specialist (Localization)

DenseNet-121 (WSOL-Tuned) · checkpoints/best_wsol_finetune.pth
  • Quantified WSOL: Mean Box-IoU 0.7631 on RSNA Pneumonia Detection (90.0% success rate vs. 0.35 target).
  • Vietnamese Clinician Grounding: Mean Box-IoU 0.6049 on VinDr-CXR (74.2% success rate) without bounding-box training.
  • Clinical Role: Fine-grained lesion iso-contour extraction and radiological spatial report grounding.

4. ResNet-50 Residual Specialist

ResNet-50 (23.5M params) · checkpoints/best_resnet50.pth
  • Deep Residual Hierarchy: Provides complementary convolutional feature representations to DenseNet-121.
  • High Specificity: Strong discriminative power on acute alveolar consolidations and pleural abnormalities.
  • Clinical Role: Second reader for cross-architecture consensus and residual feature verification.

5. Consensus Ensemble (Multi-Backbone)

DenseNet-121 + ResNet-50 + WSOL · Soft Voting & Consensus Score
  • Ensemble Agreement: Calculates inter-model consensus agreement percentage [0, 1] on every radiograph.
  • Peak AUROC: Reaches Mean AUC 0.9233 across consensus-accepted clinical predictions.
  • Clinical Role: Maximum-confidence triage for complex multi-pathology cases and clinical audit boards.
